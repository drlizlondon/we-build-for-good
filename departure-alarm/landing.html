The Traditional Trap

Panic, Snoozing & Guilt

Standard alarms treat departures like unexpected emergencies, triggering executive dysfunction loops.

  • 🚨
    Cortisol Shock Blaring sirens spike anxiety without providing a structured bridge to transition activities.
  • 🔄
    The Snooze Spiral Hitting snooze distorts time perception, feeding time blindness until you are catastrophically late.
  • ⏳
    "Just One Quick Thing" Hyperfocus locks onto an unrelated task 3 minutes before you need to leave.
  • 🔑
    The Threshold Scramble Missing keys, phone, or wallet right at the door causes panic and delayed departures.
  • 💔
    Shame & Abandonment Being 2 minutes late ruins your streak and triggers self-criticism, causing you to quit tracking.

Engineered for Focus

The 6 Core ADHD-Friendly Mechanics

Every interaction was intentionally designed around the neuroscience of executive function.

🔔
Transition Bridge

Pre-Departure Buzzer

Task-switching is notoriously exhausting for ADHD minds. Departure Alarm fires a gentle chime exactly 5 minutes before your departure time, allowing you to wrap up your current thoughts instead of suffering an abrupt jolt.

⏱️
Active Dopamine Loop

Ticking Countdown Bounty

Time blindness makes deadlines feel either "now" or "not now". Watching bonus points drop live on screen transforms abstract minutes into immediate visual stakes, triggering an irresistible urge to grab your coat early.

🌟
Zero Shame Guarantee

Stepped Forgiving Tiers

Perfectionism is a habit killer. Leaving on time earns 40 coins (up to 100 early). Up to 5 minutes late still awards 20 coins, and over 5 minutes late awards 10 coins. You never lose a streak or face a penalty screen.

🧘
Adaptive Safety Net

Grace Buffer Buttons

Real life happens: finding a missing shoe or brushing teeth takes unexpected minutes. One tap on "+2 min buffer 🧘" or "+5 min buffer 🏃" cleanly recalculates your departure target with zero guilt or shame.

🔑
Working Memory Relief

One-Tap Grab Checklist

Do not waste working memory trying to recall if you packed your essentials. Fast toggle chips for Keys, Phone, Wallet, Meds, Water, and Coat let you check items off in a single tap right on the departure screen.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting departures and beating time blindness.

Q: Does it keep my phone screen awake?

Yes! During an active departure countdown on the challenge screen, Departure Alarm activates the Screen Wake Lock API to prevent your phone from sleeping so your timer remains in view.

Q: What happens if I am late?

You are never penalized with broken streaks or shame messages. Departing up to 5 minutes late still awards 20 coins, and departures beyond 5 minutes still award 10 coins. Every departure counts!

Q: How do the grace buffer buttons work?

If putting shoes or a jacket on takes longer than planned, tap "+2 min buffer 🧘" or "+5 min buffer 🏃". The app smoothly extends your departure target time without resetting your progress.

Q: Is Departure Alarm completely free?

Yes, 100% free with zero in-app purchases, zero paywalls, zero ads, and zero sponsored popups.
